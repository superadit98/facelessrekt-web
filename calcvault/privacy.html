Privacy Policy

Calculator Vault · ag.calcvault.secure · Last updated 18 October 2026

Short version: your files never leave your device. The app shows ads, so Google receives your advertising ID and IP address. We do not run our own servers and we cannot read your vault.

1. Who we are

Calculator Vault is published by FacelessRekt Studio ("we", "us"). The controller for any personal data described below is FacelessRekt Studio, reachable at developers@facelessrekt.com.

2. What the app stores, and where

Everything you put into the vault is stored in the app's private directory on your device, in the location Android assigns to this app. No other app on a non-rooted device can read it, and it is not visible in your gallery or file manager.

  • Files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, each with its own random nonce.
  • The master key is wrapped with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 200,000 iterations, using a random 16-byte salt.
  • Your PIN is never written to disk. It is only used, at the moment you type it, to unwrap the master key.
  • Settings (auto-lock interval, screenshot-block preference) are stored as plain JSON in the same private directory. They are not secret and are not encrypted.

We do not have a copy. If you uninstall the app, the data is gone, because there is no backup and no server copy.

3. What we collect

Nothing, by us. FacelessRekt Studio operates no servers that receive app data. We have no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics SDK of our own in the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and funded by ads, served by Google AdMob. To do that, the app requests the INTERNET permission and the advertising ID. Google, as an independent controller, receives and processes:

DataPurposeRetention
Advertising IDAd selection, frequency capping, fraud preventionPer Google's policy; resettable in device settings

Google's handling of this data is governed by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete the advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads, and you can opt out of ads personalisation from the same screen. The app keeps working either way.

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, SMS, calendar, body sensors, or Bluetooth. It does not request broad file access: you pick files to import through the system picker, and the app reads only what you explicitly chose.

6. Screenshots and the recents list

While the vault is open, the app sets Android's FLAG_SECURE, which blocks screenshots and screen recording and hides the app's preview in the recent-apps switcher. The flag is cleared when you leave the vault, so the calculator behaves like any ordinary app. This is a device-side control; no data is transmitted for it.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Ads are requested with a non-child-directed tag; if you believe a child has provided data through the ad SDK, contact us and we will help you raise it with Google.

8. Data retention and deletion

Vault data lives only on your device, so deleting it is entirely in your hands: delete individual files inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything. There is no cloud copy for us to delete.

For advertising data, use Android's ad-ID controls described in section 4, or contact Google directly. If you email us, we keep the email only as long as needed to answer it.

9. Security

Encryption details are in section 2. Two honest caveats: no software protects data on a device that is already compromised at the OS level, and a short numeric PIN has a limited keyspace. PBKDF2 at 200,000 iterations makes offline guessing expensive but not impossible. Choose a longer PIN and avoid obvious sequences.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top is updated and the change is noted in the app's release notes. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
