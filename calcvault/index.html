Calculator Vault

ag.calcvault.secure

A working calculator that hides a private vault. Type your PIN, press =, and the vault opens. Type anything else and you get an ordinary calculator. No lock screen, and no hint that a PIN was ever checked.

What it does

  • PIN is 4–8 digits, entered on the calculator keypad itself.
  • A wrong PIN evaluates as a plain number. No error, no trace.
  • Optional decoy PIN opens a second, empty vault.
  • Auto-lock when you leave the app: 15s, 30s, 1m, 5m, or never.
  • Screenshots and screen recording blocked while the vault is open.
  • Stores photos, videos, audio, documents, any file.

How files are protected

  • AES-256-GCM per file, unique random nonce.
  • Master key wrapped with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 200,000 iterations, salted.
  • Changing the PIN re-wraps the key, so your files stay readable.
  • Deleting a file overwrites its data before unlinking.
  • Files live in the app's private directory. Not in your gallery.
